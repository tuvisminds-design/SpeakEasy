# 🎯 SpeakEasy Ecosystem - Complete Integration

## 🌟 Overview

**SpeakEasy** and **HireEasy** are now fully integrated into one unified ecosystem, allowing users to seamlessly switch between both applications.

## 🎨 Ecosystem Structure

### SpeakEasy (General Speaking)
- **Speech Generator** - Create speeches for any topic
- **Speaking Tips** - Master public speaking skills
- **Speech History** - Review past speeches

### HireEasy (Interview Training)
- **Upload Resume** - Start your interview journey
- **Schedule Interview** - Book your interview slot
- **Practice with SpeakEasy** - AI-powered interview training

## 🔄 Seamless Switching

### From SpeakEasy → HireEasy
- **Button Location:** After "Speech History" in sidebar
- **Label:** "Switch to HireEasy"
- **Visual:** Purple/Teal gradient highlight when active

### From HireEasy → SpeakEasy
- **Button Location:** Top-right corner of each HireEasy page
- **Label:** "Switch to SpeakEasy"
- **Visual:** Teal button with microphone icon

## 🎭 MICO - The Common Character

**MICO appears throughout both apps:**
- Guides users in HireEasy pages
- Celebrates milestones (resume upload, interview scheduled)
- Encourages practice with SpeakEasy
- Provides consistent user experience

## 🎯 User Journey

```
1. Login to SpeakEasy
   ↓
2. [Optional] Use general speaking features
   ↓
3. Click "Switch to HireEasy"
   ↓
4. Upload Resume (MICO guides)
   ↓
5. Schedule Interview (MICO celebrates)
   ↓
6. Practice with SpeakEasy (Full AI training)
   ↓
7. Click "Switch to SpeakEasy" anytime
   ↓
8. Continue with general speaking practice
```
